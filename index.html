The 6 steps

  1. 01Launch on pump.fun paired with USDC. That is the main pool.
  2. 02Dev buy about 20% of supply.
  3. 03Pair 10% with the ecosystem anchor token BTC on Meteora.
  4. 04Pair 10% with SOL on Meteora.
  5. 05The rest stays on USDC, through the curve and after graduation.
  6. 06Then partner tokens pair a chunk of their supply to the new USBD (or token of your choice). It becomes the "treasury" hub.
