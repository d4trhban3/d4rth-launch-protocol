Free launch guide

Launch a Solana coin that has real money behind it.

Most new coins start thin and die. This is a six-step way to launch so the main pool is real dollars, the sides are locked, and other coins can connect to yours. The example coin is called Bulma Dollar. You can use that idea, or write your own. Same six steps either way.

The 6 steps

  1. 01Launch the coin on pump.fun, paired with USDC. USDC is a dollar on Solana. This is the main trading pool.
  2. 02Buy about 20% of the coins yourself. That leaves most of the rest for other people.
  3. 03Lock 10% in a pool with BTC. BTC is the main coin of this group. Lock means the money cannot be pulled back out. This happens on Meteora.
  4. 04Lock 10% in a pool with SOL. Same thing, with Solana's own coin.
  5. 05Leave the rest in the USDC pool. That stays the main pool, during the launch and after.
  6. 06Then other coins lock a piece of their supply against your new coin. Your coin becomes the hub they all connect through. The example hub is Bulma Dollar. Yours can be any coin.

What these words mean

  • pump.funThe site you launch the coin on. Price goes up as people buy.
  • USDCA dollar on Solana. The main pool is priced in dollars.
  • LockPut coins in a pool and freeze them so they cannot be removed.
  • MeteoraThe site where those locked pools are made.
  • HubThe new coin that other coins connect to. Bulma Dollar is the example. Yours can be any coin.
  • Creator feeOptional. A slice of the launch fees. Holders can still get the rest.
