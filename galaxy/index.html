The collection

The main protocol is USDC · Bulma. Bulma is an example coin only. Any coin can take its place. Bulma is paired to USDC, SOL and Big Titty Coin (BTC): the main pool is USDC, 10% locks to BTC, and 10% locks to SOL. The other coins lock a chunk of their supply to Bulma.

USDT is not part of that protocol. It is an optional extra boost: add a Tether-themed token paired to USDT, Bulma and Big Titty Coin (BTC) and the same coins pull a little closer. Its USDT pool is the thinner one, so the same chunk adds less.

How the numbers work: added money = supply share × market cap (after the increase). USDT counts at about 35% of that, because Solana holds $2.40B of USDT against $6.83B of USDC. Closer = new money ÷ (current liquidity + new money), up to 42% nearer BTC.
